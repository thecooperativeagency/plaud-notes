09-16 Weekly Meeting: Lead and Service Appointment Reporting Workflow

id of_0f5509819c84a12afcd06afbfb6d5c06infer: Acquire morning lists / service+lead PDF workflow

1. Weekly Lead and Service Appointment Reporting Workflow

The team aims to streamline how salespeople receive and work lead and service appointment lists. Current pain points include fragmented sources across “so many different platforms,” duplicated or mis-categorized sold records (e.g., “Amy Lambert” vs. “Andy/Amy Lambert”), and manual printing/updating that frustrates the team. Centralizing into one customizable list mitigates inefficiency, ensures coverage of “service appointments into the future” and “old leads,” and supports targeted follow-up. Proposed solutions include a weekly Monday print and/or automated email with PDFs, configurable filters (remove solds, 90 or 180 days, “today plus future,” “all loaded days”), and tailoring to profitable trades and sweet spots (e.g., 22–24 BMWs, model search like “230/240/250”). Automation leverages existing system data and CSV report emailing via Event Solutions to agents, reducing manual overhead and increasing engagement.

Action Items

  • [ ] Set a weekly cadence to generate and distribute a consolidated list every Monday, covering service appointments for the week and active leads, with “today plus future” and “all loaded days” toggle options -- *Lance* 2026-09-21
  • [ ] Implement a “Remove Solds” filter with configurable sold-removal windows (90 or 180 days), while allowing teams to keep certain solds when appropriate -- *Lance* 2026-09-23
  • [ ] Configure an automated Monday morning email per salesperson with a PDF of their weekly service appointments -- *Lance* 2026-09-23
  • [ ] Build CSV report automation in Event Solutions to email agents directly, ensuring smooth ingestion to the app -- *Lance* 2026-09-27
  • [ ] Conduct a demo for all salespeople to drive engagement with the consolidated list and workflows -- *Lance* [Insert Name]
  • [ ] Add search/filter presets for “sweet spots,” including trades for 23 to 24, 22 to 24 BMWs, and model-specific quick searches (e.g., 230, 240, 250) -- *Lance* 2026-09-24

2. Data Model Enhancements: Statuses, Showroom Activity, and Desk Logs

Accuracy and completeness of activity capture are essential to identify re-engagement targets and consolidate lists that currently live in separate “buckets.” The team wants to include showroom visits, desk logs, and “anything we made numbers for,” alongside service and web leads, to avoid printing different lists. The solution is to compile the correct statuses indicating customers came in and were worked but not sold, bringing those into the unified report. This will support rehash opportunities and ensure recent in-store interactions are actionable without manual cross-referencing.

Action Items

  • [ ] Identify and compile statuses that indicate “customer came in,” “showroom visit,” “desk,” and “worked but not sold,” and map them into the unified reporting list -- *Lance* 2026-09-25
  • [ ] Add “Showroom visits” and “Desk” as explicit categories/filters in the app/report, ensuring they appear in the status line for list generation -- *Lance* 2026-09-26
  • [ ] Include “anything desked” and “showroom activity” from the last 30 days in the consolidated weekly list, reducing the need to print separate bucket reports -- *Lance* 2026-09-26

3. Re-Engagement Window and Profitability-Focused Filters

The team targets effective re-engagement windows based on average time to close (“twenty eight”), settling on “forty-five days” as a practical lookback. Emphasis is on focusing the list on profitable trades, rehashing trade appraisals, and filtering out less valuable items. Enhancing filters to surface “lower hanging fruit” while keeping the ability to expand ensures salespeople spend time on high-impact opportunities.

Action Items

  • [ ] Configure re-engagement lookback filters to 45 days for “worked last week” and recent activity, aligning with an average 28-day close -- *Lance* 2026-09-24
  • [ ] Add a filter to show only profitable trades and enable quick rehash workflows on those trade appraisals -- *Lance* 2026-09-24

4. Data Hygiene: Sold Badge Accuracy and Duplicate/Mislinked Records

Mislabeling solds across similar customer records (e.g., multiple “Lambert” entries) creates confusion and inaccurate reporting. The “sold badge” exists but does not resolve cross-record mismatches. Cleaning and rules to better detect and link correct customer entries will improve list fidelity and prevent reopening leads on customers just sold.

Action Items

  • [ ] Review and correct mislinked sold records (e.g., “Lambert” cases), ensuring the sold badge applies to the correct customer profile -- *Lance* 2026-09-23
  • [ ] Define matching logic or checks to reduce future mislabeling across duplicate-like names, within data already in the system -- *Lance* 2026-09-27

5. User Experience Improvements: Quick Copy and Contact Handling

Fast access to contact details supports rapid outreach. The app includes copy buttons next to each field (email, phone) to streamline contact actions. Ensuring these utilities are present and communicated to salespeople supports adoption and reduces friction during follow-up.

Action Items

  • [ ] Verify and finalize “copy” utilities for phone numbers and emails across list rows; highlight this in the sales demo -- *Lance* 2026-09-21
