  • Topic Title: Portfolio Mode vs. Normal Lead Mode in the Sales Platform

  • Topic Title: Maturity Filtering and Salesperson-Specific Views

    • - The system can filter maturities by 1, 3, 6, and 9 months and by individual salesperson portfolios. - “David Spikes” appears to have no items maturing soon in portfolio data (he has not been here long enough). - Managers want salesperson dashboards with individual logins to view only their portfolio and maturities. - There is interest in pushing selected targets into the CRM to create new opportunities. - Conclusion: Clear interest in salesperson-specific dashboards; feasibility not yet confirmed.

  • Topic Title: Integrating External Lead Sources (InfoBond, KPI, Service)

    • - The current system lists BMW KPI leads under “all ops” with adjustable date ranges. - KPI leads can overlap with service visits to check whether customers bought cars and to track engagement. - InfoBond integration is missing; InfoBond does not sync events or notes and has known limitations. - Request to explore InfoBond export to enable cross-referencing and assign portfolios in the internal system. - Some team members believe Tara’s lists include items outside the current CRM; Lance states they are the same leads already in-system. - Conclusion: KPI and service data are integrated; InfoBond remains a gap requiring investigation.

  • Topic Title: Platform and AI/Tooling Updates

    • - Podium remains only as web chat; AI functionality is now internal within Vensolutions (Vensol). - Conversations previously in Podium can be viewed by logging into Vensolutions. - Managing multiple AI projects is productive but demanding. - Conclusion: AI and chat workflows have moved to internal systems; users should access Vensolutions for conversations.

  • Topic Title: Operational Instructions Mentioned (Document Handling)
