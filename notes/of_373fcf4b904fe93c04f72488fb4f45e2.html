09-22 Consultation: Automotive Dealer Group Website and Platform Onboarding

id of_373fcf4b904fe93c04f72488fb4f45e2infer: Dealer.com group site + platform onboarding / Acquire data continuity

> Date & Time: 2026-09-22 14:06:18 > Location: [Insert Location] > Customer: [Insert Customer]

Overview

The consultation centered on website improvements for an automotive dealer group and operational alignment during onboarding to a new platform (Bayrend). The customer, Lance, manages multiple premium brands (Audi, BMW, Porsche) and oversees sales and service processes. Key topics included elevating brand visibility on the group site, replacing the static map with a dynamic “locations near you” module, clarifying Bayrend onboarding status, and safeguarding data continuity by downloading key task and opportunity information before the platform switch. Main pain points are limited brand prominence on the site, uncertainty about onboarding progress and data retention/reporting, and difficulty exporting comprehensive opportunity brief data to power Lance’s internal acquisition dashboard. Desired outcomes include enhanced UX for brand shopping and locations, clear onboarding steps, preserved access to actionable customer intelligence, and improved service-to-sales situational awareness.

Background

  • Lance represents a dealer group with Audi, BMW, and Porsche, operating multiple locations displayed on their group site.
  • A consultant (Speaker 3) proposed and will implement two site enhancements: “Shop by Brand” tiles to highlight brands and a dynamic “locations near you” section that shows address/hours and optional storefront images.
  • The group is transitioning to Bayrend; onboarding emails requesting items were received on 2026-09-21.
  • Current dashboard shows a 42% engagement rate, 20 vehicle sales (5 delivered; 4 pre-owned), 3 sales with no prior contact, 74 indirect leads, and 200 repair orders with $133,846 gross profit. Twenty-four sales appointments were set; 16 occurred; 11 resulted in sales; reported gross profit is “a little over” $54,000 on sales.
  • Lance built an internal acquisition dashboard to cross-reference service and sales leads to improve situational awareness for sales staff.

Pain Points

Limited brand prominence and suboptimal location UX on the group site reduce customer engagement and brand-driven navigation, leading to missed opportunities for customers to quickly find inventory by brand and locate nearby stores. Brand logos (Audi, BMW, Porsche) “get a little bit lost,” and the map-based location module is less dynamic. Impact: Lower click-through to brand-specific inventory and less efficient location discovery. Current situation: Static logos and a bottom-of-page map with three pins for group locations. Quantitative metrics: Not directly tied, but overall engagement rate is 42%. Examples: Customers rely on a less prominent logo section and a generic map to find locations. Context: Premium brand dealership group competing for attention; improved UI can increase conversions. Stakeholders: Marketing/website team, sales leads, customers seeking brand-specific inventory and nearest store. Uncertainty about Bayrend onboarding status and potential data loss/availability during the platform transition creates risk of workflow disruption and lost insights. Lance has “not met with them since I signed” and “doesn’t have a clue” where he is in the process until an email requesting onboarding items arrived on 2026-09-21. Impact: Potential loss of active tasks/opportunities due to “sixty-day lookback” and unclear migration, risking follow-up gaps and revenue leakage. Current situation: Consultant recommends sorting by due date and downloading reports; Lance questions whether tasks “will just stay in the report.” Quantitative metrics: Active opportunities list size is “eighty-two,” 24 appointments set, 16 transpired, 11 resulted in sales; 74 indirect leads; 200 repair orders; $133,846 service gross profit; “a little over $54,000” gross profit on sales. Examples: Orphan bucket is clean, but tasks may “peel off” when the final lever is pulled to the other platform. Context: Transition windows often have data retention rules (e.g., 60-day lookback) causing historical items to drop. Stakeholders: Lance, sales team, RSM/demo sales, Bayrend onboarding team. Inadequate export/reporting detail to support Lance’s acquisition dashboard hampers service-to-sales situational awareness. The “task detail report” only has “six rows of data” and “doesn’t tell you anything” compared to the in-app opportunity brief with customer intelligence and buying signals. Impact: Harder to identify customers in service who also have recent engagement (clicked content) and leads, reducing cross-sell opportunities and timely follow-ups. Current situation: Lance plans to “grab” the basic report and have his system name-match; considers focusing on “only the active ones” (82 records) and sorting by date. Quantitative metrics: Six rows in the downloaded report; 82 active items for potential export. Examples: Lance wants a “more complete brief” per opportunity to feed his dashboard; hyperlinks and detailed content exist in the UI but not in export. Context: Salespeople may lack situational awareness if not aggressive; Lance’s dashboard is meant to bridge that gap. Stakeholders: Lance, sales team, service advisors, customers with service appointments and leads.
