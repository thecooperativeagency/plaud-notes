Planning: Dealership Website Optimization and VCA Integration Rollout

Lance and Speaker 1 reviewed website performance metrics to address short session durations from top-funnel traffic channels. They decided to redirect promotional traffic to a specialized model research page and reviewed updated dashboard reporting. Lance also coordinated with Michael from Event Solutions to schedule Virtual Contact (VCA) training sessions for staff at three dealership locations, timing the transition to align with their existing software contract termination.

Website Traffic and Landing Page Optimization

  • Session Duration Metrics: Speaker 1 noted that the average session duration across the website was only 23 seconds. Lance stated his target for average user engagement is over three minutes, though two to three minutes is a typical benchmark. To increase session duration, the team has discussed launching a newsletter on the site and adding more vehicle photos.
  • Traffic Source Analysis: The 23-second average session duration was traced to traffic coming from Gray Media campaigns. Lance explained that Gray Media runs high-funnel awareness campaigns similar to brand-level campaigns run by Audi. This type of traffic naturally produces higher bounce rates and shorter session durations than low-funnel sources like Google Ads or retargeting campaigns, which target shoppers who are already looking for specific lease rates or vehicles.
  • Landing Page Strategy: To capture high-funnel traffic more effectively, Lance proposed changing the destination link. Instead of directing Gray Media traffic to the generic homepage, they will direct users to a long model research page that Lance previously built. This page presents multiple model options and brand entry points, which should help keep uncommitted shoppers on the site longer.
  • Traffic Volumes: Direct traffic reached 20,400 visits for the period from August 9 to September 8, 2026, matching similar trends from the previous month. Organic search continues to drive the bulk of the dealership's business, bringing in over 10,000 visits.
  • Dashboard Reporting Integration: The traffic data is pulled directly from Google Analytics 4 (GA4). Lance upgraded the GM weekly dashboard to show granular metrics for each source and medium. The updated dashboard breaks down visits, unique visitors, average session duration, and mobile actions like phone taps. Lance is currently working to filter out programmatic traffic sources that route through intermediate platforms like FlashTalking.

Virtual Contact Integration and Training

  • System Rollout and Contract Alignment: Lance is coordinating the Virtual Contact (VCA) setup across three locations: Harris Porsche, Audi Baton Rouge, and Brian Harris BMW. Lance worked with Jeff Elliman to schedule the technical transition for September 21, 2026. This date matches the end of their contract with Podium, preventing the dealerships from being double-billed for both platforms.
  • Training Session Coordination: Lance and Michael from Event Solutions agreed to schedule three separate one-hour training sessions on consecutive weekdays:

    • - Dates: Monday, September 14, Tuesday, September 15, and Wednesday, September 16, 2026. - Time: All sessions are scheduled for 2:00 PM Central (3:00 PM Eastern). This afternoon slot avoids early morning or late afternoon sales rushes. - Format: Each session is dedicated to one of the three stores, but the schedule will be shared across all locations. Staff members who cannot attend their store's assigned day due to scheduling conflicts or days off may join one of the alternate sessions. Lance plans to connect his laptop in the conference rooms to attend and facilitate all three sessions.

  • Setup and Integration Workflow: Lance scheduled a meeting with an internal team member at 1:00 PM on September 8, 2026, to begin initial VCA configurations. Lance plans to review their active Podium campaigns to ensure their historical data and specialized setups are ready for integration into Ven.
  • Opt-In Messaging Compliance: Lance and Michael discussed message compliance workflows. Podium did not require prior customer opt-in and instead included a "reply STOP to opt out" footer in text messages. Ven's default workflow involves sending an initial automated opt-in request text, which can feel unnatural to customers. Lance noted that Ven allows staff to manually check the opt-in box within the workflow, bypassing the automated opt-in text for established leads.
