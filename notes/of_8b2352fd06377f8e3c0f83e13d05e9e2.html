09-22 HP Acquire demo — sales lead generation tool

id of_8b2352fd06377f8e3c0f83e13d05e9e2infer

This conversation documents the demonstration of a new sales lead generation tool called "Acquire," developed by Lance. He explains its features, intended workflow for sales managers, and potential for future customization based on user feedback.

Introduction and Core Functionality of the "Acquire" Tool

Lance introduces "Acquire," a custom-built software tool designed to streamline lead generation for sales managers. Its primary function is to aggregate and display data from three key sources: dealership service appointments, incoming web leads, and current vehicle inventory. The core concept is to provide managers with a consolidated view of customers visiting the service department, enabling them to proactively identify sales opportunities. Lance explained that the tool was built to be a simple, efficient alternative to existing systems like ViAuto, Ven, and the more expensive Mastermind, allowing a manager to quickly "hop in" and create leads. The interface features "copy buttons" for easy data transfer to the dealership's CRM (VinSolutions). A central feature is "Export to CRM," which lets a user select a service customer, assign a potential vehicle of interest using a "lot fit" prediction based on their current car, and push the information directly into the CRM as a new lead.

Technical Demonstration and Workflow Customization

Lance conducted a live demonstration of the "Acquire" tool, showcasing its direct integration with the dealership's VIN Solutions CRM. He explained that when a lead is created through the tool, it is automatically tagged with the source "acquire service," allowing for specific tracking and process automation. During a test, they successfully exported a service customer's information, creating an active lead in the CRM almost instantly. A key discussion point arose regarding lead assignment. While the tool allows a user to select a salesperson, it was determined that the system cannot currently force-assign a lead or override the CRM's established lead-routing rules, as it is not a native Cox product and lacks that level of authorization. Instead, it adheres to the default parameters, like a standard web lead. The group also considered an alternative workflow where individual salespeople could be given access to the tool, empowering them to "filter themselves" and proactively identify opportunities from the service drive, rather than relying solely on managers to assign leads.

Feature Requests and Future Development Roadmap

The team provided several key feature requests to enhance the "Acquire" tool's utility for the Porsche-specific environment. The most significant request was to import the dealership's entire customer file to enable deep searchability. This would allow a salesperson to, for example, search for all current owners of a specific model like the Panamera or 911 when a new model arrives. Another suggestion was to add a field on the main dashboard to display the original salesperson who sold the car to the service customer, facilitating a natural and informed follow-up. The group also discussed the need to digitize and integrate physical lead sheets provided by Porsche Financial Services (PFS). Lance affirmed his ability to implement these features rapidly and positioned the users as early adopters in a beta phase.

Data Sourcing and Integration Challenges

PCNA and PFS operate as distinct legal entities and do not share data due to privacy laws. Lease maturity and finance leads arrive on paper, not as digital files. Agreed workaround: store scans monthly physical PFS lead sheets and emails the PDF to Lance; Ada extracts and ingests.
