Overview

The consultation centers on configuring the Virtual Contact Assistant (VCA), named “Kaylee,” for Lance’s automotive dealerships (Porsche, Audi, BMW). Initial rollout starts with Porsche (ID: 21013). The goals are to align Kaylee’s behavior with the current Podium AI—setting appointments and engaging customers without confusion—while refining persona (human vs. virtual), pricing disclosure, texting hours, tone, and re-engagement logic. Key focus areas include preventing conversation restarts caused by frequent “Notify Assistant” steps, streamlining migrated instructions from Podium to fit platform constraints, and tailoring brand-specific rules (e.g., Porsche MSRP vs. Audi Internet Price). Final advanced options, process customizations, and suppression rules will be completed with Project Manager Gage, with activation planned in about five days.

Background

Lance manages configurations for Porsche, Audi, and BMW dealerships and is transitioning from Podium to Kaylee. The initial configuration targets Harish Porsche, with plans to replicate optimized settings across Audi and BMW. The team also uses Predictive Insights; Gen AI availability varies by store (Harish Porsche lacks Gen AI). The meeting is a follow-up to review and refine settings, including advanced options and special instructions migrated from Podium, before a final session with Gage and full rollout next week.

Pain Points

  • Conversation resets: Each “Notify Assistant” step relaunches a fresh conversation, causing loss of context and repetitive customer experiences. Daily and periodic notifications (days 1–6, 9, 11, 15, 22) frequently re-start threads. Auto-pause is overridden by subsequent notifications unless suppression is applied.
  • Persona and operational rules: Uncertainty about presenting Kaylee as human vs. virtual assistant affects customer perception, error handling, and stakeholder alignment across brands.
  • After-hours messaging compliance: Desire for 24/7 responsiveness conflicts with unknown state-specific regulations, creating legal risk for late-night automated texts.
  • Instruction bloat and character limits: Migrated Podium content includes redundant details (address, hours, tone) already covered by system settings. The 1500-character limit constrains inclusion of more critical rules, risking inefficiency and inconsistency.
  • Service inquiry handling: Prior tools were bogged down by service questions; Lance wants to ensure Kaylee avoids service topics or routes appropriately.
  • Cross-store configuration clarity: Some settings can be copied; others (pricing permissions, brand-specific custom instructions) must be set individually. Gen AI capability inconsistencies complicate rollout.

Expectations

  • Align Kaylee’s behavior with Podium: actively manage interactions, set general appointments for test drives/appraisals without committing to specific vehicle availability, and stay engaged unless manually paused by sales.
  • Brand-specific pricing rules: Porsche to disclose MSRP; Audi to disclose Internet Price with discounts highlighted.
  • Controlled communication parameters: Configure texting hours and tone (“friendly,” greet by name), ensure compliance with state regulations, and define re-engagement logic (currently set for ninety days of inactivity).
  • Streamlined instruction set: Remove redundant information to free space for critical, brand-specific rules (e.g., out-of-state buyers, EV/hybrid specifics).
  • Process ownership: PM Gage to copy/create AI-integrated internet sales flows and finalize advanced options, suppressions, and re-engagement rules across all stores before go-live.
