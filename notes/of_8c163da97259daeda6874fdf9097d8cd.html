Overview

This consultation summary covers a discussion between Lance and Maddie Savoy of Kip and King Marketing regarding social media content creation services for car dealerships. Lance is seeking high-quality, cinematic video and photography rather than "goofy" viral-style content. Topics included content types, pricing, and Lance’s need for content creation only—no social media management—since he already uses a syndicated posting system. The core challenge is aligning the agency’s standard package pricing with a content-only request.

Background

Lance, who manages or consults for car dealerships, is evaluating Kip and King Marketing’s services. He uses an automated system that posts to social channels via Google Drive, so he does not want full social media management. He strongly prefers cinematic-style video (e.g., Ferrari dealership examples) over trendy or “dumb” viral content. He aims to understand the value and pricing for content creation he can feed into his existing system.

Pain Points

The primary issue is the misalignment between the agency’s bundled service and Lance’s content-only needs. His syndicated posting system makes management/posting redundant. He stated, “I don’t need it posted or managed... that really is just literal content creation.” Paying $1,750 per month for unused services creates a value concern. The agency’s initial stance that pricing would remain unchanged does not address his need, pushing him toward an ill-suited package.

Expectations

Lance wants a customized, content-only pricing model. He expects a set number of high-quality photos and video reels per month delivered via Google Drive for his system to handle. He is interested in roughly 20 assets monthly, including one video reel per week (four per month), and seeks a fair price solely for asset creation—excluding management, scheduling, and posting. Success is a quote reflecting this reduced scope.

Other Information Summary

  • Kip and King Marketing has an in-house photographer and videographer who travel for shoots.
  • The standard package is $1,750 per month per dealership.
  • It includes one site visit per month and delivers about five posts per week, including photography, graphics, and one video reel weekly.
  • Maddie Savoy is local to Lance’s area.
  • Lance prefers high-quality, cinematic content over “goofy” or “dumb” viral videos, while acknowledging that style works for some dealerships (e.g., one in Texas).
