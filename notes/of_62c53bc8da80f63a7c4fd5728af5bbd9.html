  • It was not overwhelming, but there were customers and a few potential deals to follow up on.

Development of the "Acquire" App

  • Lance introduced a new app, "Acquire," designed to consolidate lead sources into a single view.
  • The app integrates service appointments, AFS (Audi Financial Services) leads, dealer and web leads, and phone ups.
  • It addresses the issue of opportunity data being spread across systems like Viano and Ven.
  • Users can filter for the service drive, identify service appointments that already have associated web leads, and check if they are sold opportunities.

"Acquire" App Features and Functionality

  • The app highlights "sweet spot" vehicles (e.g., model years 2022–2024) desirable for trade-ins.
  • Users can export service appointments directly to the CRM as new leads and assign them to a salesperson.
  • The system can suggest the "obvious next car" for a customer, which Lance calls "lot fit".
  • This is similar to "Mastermind" but is a simpler, more focused tool the company doesn’t have to pay for.
  • "Mastermind" is still used due to direct monetary incentives from the manufacturer.

Future Enhancements for the "Acquire" App

  • Speaker 3 requested adding the vehicle’s original purchase date to avoid contacting recent buyers about trades.
  • Another suggestion was linking Carfax reports to show registration dates; Lance will investigate this.
  • Lance will add more data awareness for cars in service to identify better opportunities.
  • Lance mentioned a separate AI process being built for VCA to handle AFS leads, including offering pre-inspections.
