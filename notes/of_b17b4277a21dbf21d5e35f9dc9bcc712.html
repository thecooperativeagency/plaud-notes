09-15 Lecture: Virtual Contact Assistant Training for Sales and Managers

id of_b17b4277a21dbf21d5e35f9dc9bcc712inferred

VCA staff training (VinSolutions) — Michael Pratt

Tue Sep 15 lecture for sales + managers on Virtual Contact Assistant (“Vanessa”).

What it is: Behind-the-scenes AI that engages customers, books appointments, gathers info. When she can’t handle a request (discount, trade, credit, etc.), she creates a needs-help task for the salesperson.

Sales play:

  • Live on the task page; find the Vanessa banner
  • Open customer profile → review Vanessa thread (don’t rely on generic “needs help” alone)
  • Contact the customer, then Complete the task — never Dismiss (reads as ignored)
  • Sentiment colors are keyword-ish; always read the thread
  • Pause 1–30 days when customer asks no AI / vacation / negative thread; managers resume from profile or pause list

Managers: Live view of VA emails/texts; Insight Virtual Assistant Dashboard; Settings → Dealer Settings → Virtual Assistant Settings (pause list).

Config notes from room: VA often has its own text line vs dealership SMS (confirmed pattern on Harris Porsche in-session). Appointments follow store hours.

Dest: dealers (BH group VCA path). Disposition: park — training reference for Sep 21 cutover; no new build WO.
