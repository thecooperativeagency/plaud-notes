1. Vanessa AI Bot Configuration and Issue Resolution

The team assessed multiple customer service issues caused by Vanessa, the new AI solution bot. Customers and sales staff reported that Vanessa sends text messages at inappropriate hours (e.g., 2:00 AM or 3:00 AM), messages too frequently (every 10 to 15 minutes), fails to retain conversation context, sets duplicate appointments or creates appointments without customer confirmation resulting in no-shows, and provides incorrect inventory information (such as stating X5 vehicles were in stock and available for sale on 2026-10-15 when they were not). Additionally, the AI continues messaging customers when salespeople text directly from personal phones instead of through the Vin application, bypassing the automatic engagement shut-off. Planned remedies include adjusting messaging windows, enforcing frequency limits, educating salespeople to message inside Vin or use the pause feature (up to 30 days), and escalating backend system flaws to technical support.
