Meeting Notes

Recall Campaign Strategy for Non-DMS Customers

  • Proposal: Contact all customers not in the DMS to bring their cars in for recalls.
  • Concern: Older models (e.g., VANOS, water pump) pose higher breakage risk during repairs.
  • Parameters discussed: Set a hard stop by model year to mitigate risk.
  • Decision: Limit campaign to vehicles from model year 2015 and newer.
  • Outreach channels: Email, mail, text, and voice messages targeting ~2,700 nearby BMW owners.
  • Service scope: Include appointment scheduling, functioning as a BDC for recall-related services.

Website Service Coupon Discussion

  • Goal: Implement a 10% off service coupon on the company website to attract customers.
  • Max discount debated: $150 or $200 initially; prior Epsilon email blasts used $100 max.
  • Competitor review: Brian Harris’s site appears to offer 10% with no stated maximum, posing potential risk.
  • Focus: Define clear exclusions for the offer.
  • Conclusion: Add a 10% off service coupon with a $100 maximum discount; exclusions include value service, tires, and glass.
