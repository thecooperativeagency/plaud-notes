09-02 Auto Dealership's Customer Re-engagement Stalled by Podium Platform Misuse

Core Synopsis

The dealership's customer re-engagement operation is functionally stalled because the team lacks the basic ability to extract a targeted contact list from its own messaging platform. Speaker 2 sent a "Been a While" loyalty template to an estimated 200–300 former Audi customers — largely legacy accounts from a salesperson named Ryan — and now needs to personally reach out to that specific cohort via personal cell phone as the dealership phases out of a product line. The operational blocker is not the outreach itself, but the inability to filter Podium's inbox to surface that precise recipient list. Compounding this, the conversation exposed a deeper systemic failure: Podium messages are being buried without resolution — a customer had been waiting three weeks for a reply — because the team has not adopted the platform's filtering and task tools with any discipline. Lance pointed to the "Engaged" filter and the task list as the correct triage mechanisms, but the team is clearly not using either consistently. We are sitting on a communication infrastructure that nobody has actually learned to operate, and customers are falling through the gaps because of it.

---

The Podium Competency Gap: Buried Leads and a Broken Retrieval Workflow

1. The Immediate Operational Need

Speaker 2 requires a filtered export of all contacts who received the "Been a While" template message — an estimated 200–300 recipients, predominantly former Audi owners previously handled by Ryan. The intent is to transition outreach to personal cell phone contact as the dealership exits a specific product or service line. The Podium platform holds this data, but neither Speaker 2 nor Lance immediately knew the precise export or filter path to retrieve it during the conversation.

2. The Systemic Inbox Problem

The conversation surfaced a structural failure in how Podium is being used as a daily triage tool:

  • Messages are going unanswered for weeks. Speaker 2 cited a customer who waited three weeks for a reply — a direct consequence of replies being buried beneath high-volume outbound blast threads.
  • The "Engaged" filter is the correct tool but unused. Lance identified the Engaged filter as the mechanism to surface contacts who have replied and are awaiting a response. This was apparently unknown or underutilized by the primary daily user.
  • The Task List is not being maintained. Lance referenced the Podium task list as the intended follow-up safety net. Speaker 2's experience contradicts its effectiveness in practice — the three-week lapse suggests tasks are either not being created or not being checked.

3. The Opt-In Friction Point

Speaker 2 expressed reluctance to route outreach through Podium due to the opt-in confirmation message it sends to contacts. Lance clarified that the opt-in checkbox can be pre-selected before sending to suppress that prompt, and that recipients would only receive a standard opt-out notice. For a list of established repeat customers, this distinction is operationally significant and removes the stated barrier to using the platform for follow-up.
