Objectives include replicating and enhancing Podium’s nuanced, campaign-specific messaging for varied lead types (e.g., manufacturer lease maturity/NCI vs. standard web leads), enabling natural initial SMS via the new Established Business Relationship (EBR) setting, and implementing role-based guardrails, tone/persona customization, and dealership differentiators. A controlled rollout is targeted before the “go dark” date on September 21, followed by monitoring and iterative optimization, including portfolio-style engagement for NCI lease leads. Background

  • Lance, a seasoned consultant supporting the three dealerships, seeks full adoption and a seamless transition by September 21.
  • All stores currently use Podium; the integrated VCA GenAI will replace it. BMW has Predictive Insights (underused), and all three stores have access to the Insights VCA dashboard (attempted/actual contacts, appointments, active conversations).

    • Pain Points

  • Replicating Podium’s nuanced, lead-type-specific messaging: BMW FS/NCI vs. Audi web leads require distinct scripts and rules to avoid generic outreach and awkward first messages.
  • Initial opt-in friction: Default VCA opt-in as the first SMS feels unnatural; EBR should enable immediate, relevant conversation for inquiry-based leads.
  • Guardrails and conversation policies: No predefined rules for sensitive topics (e.g., pricing), risking misaligned conversations and compliance issues without explicit hand-offs to sales.
  • Reporting access and visibility: Permission settings risk oversharing; managers need dashboard access while limiting reps’ visibility to all customers.
  • Process design complexity: Timing, suppressions, and task sequencing must be tuned to avoid misfires, duplication, and clutter.
  • NCI strategy uncertainty: Limited precedent for VCA engaging manufacturer lease maturity leads; differences in OEM feeds complicate routing and tone.
  • Duplicate after-hours messaging: Podium and VCA both sending messages created confusion; after-hours VCA was disabled across stores as a temporary fix.
  • Adoption and training risk: Managers and reps need clear guidance on pause lists, inbox/notes visibility, sentiment interpretation, and escalation to ensure consistent use.

    • Expectations

  • Fully replicate and surpass Podium’s capabilities by September 21, including tailored flows for distinct lead types and natural initial SMS via EBR.
  • Configure tone/persona, dealership differentiators, and explicit guardrails (e.g., pricing escalation to sales) prior to go-live; measure appointments, engagement quality, and reduced escalations.
  • Establish reporting permissions so managers access dashboards while restricting rep overexposure; verify Insights favorites and data accuracy.
  • Enable and tune Internet and showroom follow-up processes with minute-based offsets, suppressions, and clear task wording; copy processes to accelerate setup.
  • Pilot NCI engagement with portfolio-manager voice (pre-inspection, supportive messaging) for BMW 270-day lease leads and Audi source-coded leads; iterate based on early results.
  • Conduct role-based training, reinforce adoption, and monitor pause lists and sentiment 2–3 times weekly; schedule pre–September 21 final check and early October review.
