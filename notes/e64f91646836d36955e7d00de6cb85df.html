09-02 Misconfigured AI Clock-Stop Policy in VinSolutions Risks Masking Unworked Leads at Audi Baton Rouge

id e64f91646836d36955e7d00de6cb85dfinferred

Core Synopsis

The clock-stop policy in VinSolutions (VCA) is mis-specified for Audi Baton Rouge’s transition from Podium to Vens AI: automated replies from “Chloe” are stopping the response timer without a visible distinction from human responses, creating false compliance signals and masking unworked leads. The Sales Operations team changed the assistant’s display name to Chloe, confirming intent to keep AI in the loop, but the current configuration blurs accountability between AI and humans and risks management misreading SLA adherence while customers haven’t received a human follow-up. We are responsible for preventing metric gaming and ensuring operational clarity; the system must show when the clock is stopped by AI versus a human, and Podium must be prevented from affecting the clock during the transition to avoid double-stopping or misleading timestamps.

VCA Configuration Gap and Transition Risks

The Operational Gap: AI vs. Human Response Distinction

  • Phenomenon: Automated VCA (Chloe) responses can stop the lead response clock in VinSolutions.
  • Mechanism: Current settings treat AI replies as equivalent to human responses for SLA timing.
  • Consequence: Management dashboards may show timely responses while no human has engaged, risking customer experience and accountability drift.

Transition Complexity: Podium Deactivation and Overlap Control

  • Status: Team is moving off Podium to Vens AI; residual Podium behavior may still stop clocks.
  • Risk: Dual systems could both stop or manipulate clock states, compounding tracking errors and obscuring true response ownership during the cutover.

Governance Requirement: Visibility and Accountability

  • Need: A clear indicator in VinSolutions that differentiates “Clock stopped by Chloe (AI)” versus “Clock stopped by Human,” plus a field or flag that shows “Awaiting human follow-up.”
  • Stakeholder: Gage (new VinSolutions owner) is the control point to validate configuration options, field labels, and workflow automation to preserve SLA integrity.
