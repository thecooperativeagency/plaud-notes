09-04 Consultation: Brian Harris BMW Marketing Automation Transition to Fullpath CDP, Audience Activation, and Chat

id cec6fa46aca9dca917b3a7b116ac38a3inferred

> Date & Time: 2026-09-04 13:00:45 / 2026-09-04 13:00:45 > Location: [Insert Location] > Customer: Lance (Brian Harris BMW)

Overview

The consultation focused on transitioning Brian Harris BMW from their current marketing automation platform (AMP) to Fullpath’s Audience Activation and Customer Data Platform (CDP). The customer, Lance, is also moving off Podium and needs to replace its chat and review functionalities. The primary pain points are the inefficiency of the current AMP system generating unqualified leads, limited data breadth for precise targeting, underutilization of service campaigns, and the manual effort required to move slow inventory. The vendor presented how Fullpath’s Audience Activation, powered by an enriched CDP, would use broader data (including offsite shopping behavior from AutoTrader/KBB, Equifax data, etc.) to drive omni-channel campaigns, improve targeting, and deliver real-time shopper insights. The discussion also covered Fullpath's dynamic chat tool and its ability to re-engage past customers ("defectors") for an associated Audi dealership. Desired outcomes include a more efficient, integrated system that qualifies leads before creating tasks, improves ROI, enables more precise targeting, enhances service/recall engagement, accelerates inventory movement, and provides access to unified analytics.

Background

  • Customer Context: Brian Harris BMW is currently using an older version of AMP, managed by Lance, who keeps about thirty default campaigns live with 2–4 ad hoc monthly updates (APR/lease offers, slow-moving inventory). The dealership is also ceasing its use of Podium for review invites, AI BDC, and website chat.
  • Performance: The current AMP has shown strong ROI, generating “twenty sales for eighty-four grand” and “three hundred and eighty-two repair orders for two hundred and ninety-eight thousand dollars” in the last 60 days.
  • Proposal: Replace AMP with Fullpath Audience Activation at the same monthly cost ($1,900/month), with the CDP provided free for 60 days (then optional at $9.99/month). A new dynamic chat tool is also available for $900/month. The new platform leverages a CDP to integrate data from Cox products, CRM, DMS, inventory feeds, VAuto, websites, and ads, enriched with Equifax data for accurate contact/equity information.
  • Tools: Fullpath offers omni-channel (email/SMS) outreach with FTC-compliant AI-generated content, a Virtual Customer Assistant (VCA), and the Omni analytics platform for insights into sales, marketing, and service performance.
  • Group Opportunity: An associated Audi store is resistant to the old AMP but could be pitched the new Fullpath platform as a replacement for its Mastermind software to target defected customers.

Pain Points

  • Inefficient Lead Generation & Wasted Time: The primary pain point is that the current AMP creates tasks indiscriminately for any marketing interaction, overwhelming the sales team with low-quality follow-ups on non-qualified leads. This "bullshit" behavior wastes time, causes staff to manually "fish" for real opportunities, and leads to missed revenue.
  • Limited Targeting Precision & Data Breadth: The current AMP setup relies on basic, static campaigns ("nothing fancy") running off internal data, lacking the broader, CDP-level insights from offsite shopper behavior. This limits personalization and the ability to engage shoppers dynamically across channels.
  • Disjointed Tools & Fragmented Reporting: Lance is moving off Podium and needs an integrated replacement for its chat and review functions. He has also built custom dashboards because existing reporting tools are "brutal" and make it "really hard to see all that in one place," indicating a need for a unified analytics view.
  • Underutilization of Service & Recall Campaigns: The store “doesn’t do a lot of service” campaign activation, leading to missed revenue and retention opportunities. Campaigns for tires, brakes, service page abandonment, and recalls are not fully leveraged.
  • Customer Defection at Sister Store: The Audi dealership has a significant problem with customer defection due to slow product cycles. They need a tool to effectively run re-engagement campaigns targeting customers from up to six years ago, as their current tool (Mastermind) is insufficient.
  • Manual Management of Slow Inventory: Lance gives "a little extra love" to slow-moving "problem child" inventory via ad hoc monthly updates. This is a manual process that lacks integrated behavioral signals to proactively identify and target high-intent shoppers for these specific VINs.
  • Lack of Rapid Lead Injection Capability: The dealership lacks an agile process to inject high-propensity leads into the CRM on-demand to meet end-of-month sales targets.

Expectations

  • Improve Lead Quality and Sales Efficiency: The primary expectation is that Fullpath will intelligently qualify leads via its VCA, only creating tasks for genuine "hand raisers." Success will be measured by a reduction in wasted sales time and increased conversions from marketing efforts.
  • Achieve Better ROI with Broader Data: Leverage the CDP-driven Audience Activation to outperform AMP on the same budget ($1,900/month). Success metrics include higher open rates (targeting 84%+), increased sales/service ROI, and faster movement of slow VINs. The CDP will be trialed for 60 days.
  • Consolidate Tools with an Integrated Platform: Find a comprehensive, integrated replacement for Podium's services, specifically a more advanced chat tool that is BMW co-op eligible. Lance also expects robust, consolidated reporting and the ability to schedule automated data exports to feed his existing structures.
  • Expand Service and Recall Outreach: Activate a robust set of OEM-compliant service campaigns (op codes, A/B service, page abandon, recall) to increase ROs beyond the current 382 per 60 days and drive service-to-sale conversions.
  • Win Over the Resistant Audi Dealership: Successfully re-pitch the "drastically different" Fullpath platform to the Audi GM, demonstrating its ability to replace Mastermind and effectively target defected customers to win their business.
  • Enable Proactive Inventory Management & Month-End Lead Injection: Use the CDP/Omni to identify slow VINs and target high-intent shoppers. Establish a "Data Ups" process to push high-AIM (≥8), high-credit (≥750) prospects into the CRM to meet month-end targets.

Other Information Summary

  • Pricing: Audience Activation is $1,900/month. The contract is month-to-month. The CDP is free for the first 60 days, then $9.99/month. The new chat tool is $900/month and is BMW co-op eligible.
  • Group Store Offer: Other group stores can be "grandfathered in" at the same price if they transition within 30 days; otherwise, the cost is about $5,000 per store.
  • Compliance & Optimization: Email/SMS content is AI-generated and FTC/OEM compliant. The system uses send-time optimization to deliver messages when customers are most likely to open them.
  • Data Enrichment & Scale: The platform uses Equifax to enrich customer data (contact info, garage, equity). A Chicago store example cited 8 million data points processed in one week, identifying 192,000 unique shoppers.
  • Onboarding: The transition to the new platform is estimated to take about one week. The vendor's team will handle the co-op submission process with BMW.
