Proper filament routing required feeding the strand from underneath the spool holder into the intake mechanism. Multi-material parameters were verified, confirming that PLA and PETG can run within the same print job provided the nozzle temperature is maintained between 230°C and 250°C. Automatic purge cycles deposited excess waste filament ("poop") during material priming and dynamic flow testing.

The users elected to execute an initial test print of a household chip clip (using 4 grams of matte black PLA), completing in roughly 16 minutes to verify print fidelity.
